**SQL generated by Genie**

```sql
WITH `equipment_metrics` AS (
  SELECT
    `equipment`,
    MEASURE(`unplanned_downtime_hours`) AS `unplanned_downtime_hours`,
    MEASURE(`total_maintenance_cost`) AS `total_maintenance_cost`
  FROM `fr_stable_catalog`.`supply_chain`.`mv_reliability`
  WHERE `equipment` IS NOT NULL
  GROUP BY ALL
),
`downtime_ranked` AS (
  SELECT
    `equipment`,
    `unplanned_downtime_hours`,
    RANK() OVER (ORDER BY `unplanned_downtime_hours` DESC) AS `downtime_rank`
  FROM `equipment_metrics`
),
`cost_ranked` AS (
  SELECT
    `equipment`,
    `total_maintenance_cost`,
    RANK() OVER (ORDER BY `total_maintenance_cost` DESC) AS `cost_rank`
  FROM `equipment_metrics`
)
SELECT
  'highest_unplanned_downtime' AS `metric`,
  `equipment`,
  `unplanned_downtime_hours` AS `value`
FROM `downtime_ranked`
WHERE `downtime_rank` = 1
UNION ALL
SELECT
  'highest_maintenance_cost' AS `metric`,
  `equipment`,
  `total_maintenance_cost` AS `value`
FROM `cost_ranked`
WHERE `cost_rank` = 1
```

## 15. [contrast-2 (follow-up)] and what does OEE mean, and why do all three components matter?

*Agent:* Manufacturing and Reliability · *status:* COMPLETED

**Answer**

OEE means Overall Equipment Effectiveness, and in this data it is tracked in `fr_stable_catalog`.`supply_chain`.`mv_manufacturing` as the combination of availability, performance, and quality. Availability reflects uptime, performance reflects running speed versus target, and quality reflects good output versus defects. All three matter because weak performance in any one of them lowers total OEE even if the other two are strong.
